# README: AE-Interactive-Community-Map

**Link:** [https://scarlettmsj9-svg.github.io/AE-Interactive-Community-Map/](https://scarlettmsj9-svg.github.io/AE-Interactive-Community-Map/)

## Description

This interactive map visualizes the geographical distribution of Arts Etobicoke contributors alongside community context, including neighbourhood income levels, Toronto’s transit network, and our two galleries.

To protect privacy, the data is anonymized. Contributors are identified by a contributor ID, and their 6-digit postal codes are simplified to 3-digit postal codes.

This map is intended to help us understand where our contributors come from, how they engage with us, and what their involvement journeys look like. This map is a work-in-progress version.

## How to Run

* **Open link:** [https://scarlettmsj9-svg.github.io/AE-Interactive-Community-Map/](https://scarlettmsj9-svg.github.io/AE-Interactive-Community-Map/)

## Key Features

* The map legend on the top-left shows what each color and line represents.
* The map shows AE’s 2 galleries of storefront and cloverdale mall with blue ink icons. It also shows 'Etobicoke', 'Cloverdale', 'Islington', and 'Rexdale' position, which are the communities we engage very much with.
* **Contributor clusters & dots:**
  * 1 small point = 1 contributor. In the default setting of the map, the points with numbers on it shows how many contributors there are in this region. The more contributors in this area, the redder the point is.
  * Zoom in (using mouse scroll or the +/- buttons) to see individual contributors as small single dots.
  * You can click on each point and see this contributor profile with their roles and their engagement track to see their histories with us.
  * Bottom-left artist rank shows the most engaged artists. You can click in each row and it navigates you to each artist, and you can see their contributor profile.
* The top-right legend with checkbox enables users to choose the layers they would like to see, like income polygon, or transportation lines, etc.

## Data Source

* **Neighbourhood income:** Statistics Canada, 2021 Census (Census metropolitan areas (CMAs), tracted census agglomerations (CAs) and census tracts (CTs)) Census Profile Downloads, 2021
* **Cycling network:** City of Toronto Open Data Cycling Network - City of Toronto Open Data Portal
* **TTC bus routes:** Layer: TTC Bus Route (ID: 10)
* **GO transit & TTC subway:** overpass turbo
* **Contributor data:** Desensitized-Selected+Registered+Transaction Data Spreadsheet
